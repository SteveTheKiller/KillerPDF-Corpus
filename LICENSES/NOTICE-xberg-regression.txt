xberg test_documents regression corpus

This import contains 204 arXiv papers and 98 GovDocs1 files selected from the
vendored lane of the xberg-io test_documents regression corpus. Eight additional
GovDocs1 files in the pinned source manifest were exact duplicates of files
already in KillerPDF Test Library and were not copied again.

The imported arXiv files retain their individual Creative Commons licenses:
192 use Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, 11 use CC0 1.0
Universal, and 1 uses Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. Exact
file-level license and evidence fields are recorded in import-manifest.csv and
the complete upstream provenance table is preserved in the pinned source
archive.

The imported GovDocs1 files are works of the United States government and are
in the public domain in the United States under 17 U.S.C. 105. Their exact
file-level source and evidence records are preserved in import-manifest.csv and
the pinned source archive.
